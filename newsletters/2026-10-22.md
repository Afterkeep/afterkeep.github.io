## This week's tip: don't cancel the mobile number straight away

When someone dies, cancelling their phone plan feels like an easy first job. Hold off. Many banks, email providers and government services send sign-in or recovery codes by SMS, so keeping the number active while accounts are closed through official processes can make the admin much easier. Agree with the executor before using any code to get into an account, and close things through each provider's proper process.

## Worth knowing: OneDrive has its own "Digital legacy" setting

Microsoft's general position is that it won't release a deceased person's Outlook.com or OneDrive content without a valid subpoena or court order. But OneDrive has a separate, opt-in feature called Digital legacy that many people don't know about. According to Microsoft's support page:

- You invite one trusted contact (they need a Microsoft account) from OneDrive's Settings, under Digital legacy.
- Once they accept, you get a code you can share now, write into your will, or give to your executor. The code doesn't change or expire.
- When they request access with the code, there's a 72-hour wait, then they get read-only access to your OneDrive files and photos. It doesn't cover Outlook email.
- If it was never set up, Microsoft says it's generally unable to give information to non-account holders.

Source: [Preserve your digital legacy with OneDrive (Microsoft Support)](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onedrive/preserve-your-digital-legacy-with-onedrive), and [Accessing Microsoft services when someone has died (Microsoft Support)](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/manage/accessing-outlook-com-onedrive-and-other-microsoft-services-when-someone-has-died). ## Featured tool: Google Inactive Account Manager

*Editor's pick, not sponsored. Afterkeep isn't paid for this mention.*

If you use Gmail, Google Photos or Drive, this free setting is worth ten minutes. You choose an inactivity period, pick up to 10 people, and decide which data types each of them gets via a download link. You can also choose to have the account deleted. Without it, Google says it may delete an account that's been inactive across Google for at least two years.
