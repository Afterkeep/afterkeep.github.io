## This week's tip: set up one "if I'm gone" tool today

Pick your most important account (usually email) and switch on its built-in legacy tool: Google's Inactive Account Manager, Apple's Legacy Contact or similar. It takes about five minutes and stops your family being locked out of the account everything else resets through.

## Worth knowing: Google's Inactive Account Manager

Google's help page says Inactive Account Manager lets you share parts of your account data, or notify someone, if your account is inactive for a period you choose:

- You can pick up to 10 people and share all or only specific data types, with different data for different people.
- Google looks at signals such as last sign-ins, My Activity, Gmail app use and Android check-ins to judge inactivity.
- The plan only triggers after the inactivity period you set; some information can't be shared.

Source: [About Inactive Account Manager (Google Account Help)](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546). Checked 26 November 2026.
