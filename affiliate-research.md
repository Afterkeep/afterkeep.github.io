# Afterkeep affiliate program research
Researched 15 Oct 2026 (AEST). Official pages were used for terms wherever possible. Lines marked "(third-party)" come from secondary directories and must be checked once approved. Nothing has been signed up for.

General notes for Benno (Australia):
- Impact, CJ, PartnerStack and Proton's Tune portal all accept non-US publishers. Each needs: name/legal entity, address, website URL + description of traffic, **tax form W-8BEN** (non-US individual, claim AU treaty) or W-8BEN-E for a company, and payout details (bank/PayPal/Payoneer). ABN optional but useful.
- Site is new with low traffic. Most programs review the site manually; a live, content-rich site with an affiliate disclosure page improves approval odds. Expect some rejections or a request to reapply later.
- Disclosure: add a site-wide affiliate disclosure plus a "We may earn a commission" label next to affiliate links (rel="sponsored nofollow"). ACCC/FTC-compliant.
- Most of these programs are US-centric products, but they sell online worldwide, apart from Trust & Will, which is US law only.

## What needs Benno's details
Every application needs Benno personally, because they're legally binding agreements:
- Legal name, address, phone, email (Afterkeep800@gmail.com is fine as contact)
- Tax: W-8BEN (individual) or W-8BEN-E + ABN (if a company/sole-trader business)
- Payout: bank account (Proton, CJ, Impact), PayPal (PartnerStack/Impact), BTC address (Ledger)
- Agreement acceptance and traffic numbers (currently new, low traffic, so be honest)
